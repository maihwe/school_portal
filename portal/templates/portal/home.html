About our school

An education that goes beyond the classroom.

Edor Peter's Memorial Secondary School is a purpose-driven learning community located at Ogore-Ito in Obi Local Government Area of Benue State, along Oju L.G. Road. The school exists to give young people a dependable academic foundation, strong moral direction and the confidence required to succeed in a rapidly changing world. We believe that quality education should develop the whole child. For that reason, our approach combines serious classroom teaching with discipline, personal attention, leadership development, creativity and responsible citizenship.

From Junior Secondary School through Senior Secondary School, students are guided by committed teachers who understand that every learner has different strengths and possibilities. Lessons are structured to build genuine understanding, good study habits and readiness for important examinations including BECE, WAEC and NECO. Beyond examination success, students are encouraged to communicate clearly, think independently, work respectfully with others and solve practical problems. Parents and guardians are valued as essential partners, with our school portal helping families stay connected to results, fee records and learning materials.

Our location provides a focused and welcoming environment for families within Ogore-Ito, Obi, Oju and neighbouring communities. We are committed to maintaining a school culture in which learners feel safe, respected, challenged and inspired to improve. Choosing Edor Peter's Memorial Secondary School means choosing an institution that takes academic progress, good character and every child's future seriously. We invite parents to visit, speak with our admissions team and discover a school where potential is recognised, effort is celebrated and students are prepared not only to pass examinations, but to become useful, confident and principled members of society.
